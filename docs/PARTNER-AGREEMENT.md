# Working with realtor and lender partners

How the real-estate and mortgage leads turn into money for you, and how you stay protected. **Not legal advice:** have a South Carolina attorney review the agreement below before you sign anything, and have each partner run it past their broker-in-charge or compliance team.

## How it works (automatic delivery)

```
Website form ──► automated checks (bot check, real phone/email, 18+, no duplicates) ──► score A/B/C
                                        │
          partner covers that city, takes that tier, is under their cap,
          has prepaid balance, and is NAMED in that page's consent box?
                    │ yes                                   │ no
                    ▼                                       ▼
   delivered instantly to ONE partner:              kept by CPP Holdings LLC
   email + portal + their booking link              (you work it or leave it)
   balance drops by the lead price
```

- **Each city page names the partners who cover that city** in its consent box ("CPP Holdings LLC and Jane Smith of ABC Realty"). That's the consumer's permission for Jane to call. The router will never send a lead to someone that page didn't name.
- **You don't call or vet anything.** The form, the checks and the scoring do that. You set each partner's areas, price, weekly cap and minimum tier once, in **Admin → Partners**.
- **One lead, one partner.** Leads are exclusive. When several partners cover a city, they take turns (the one who has waited longest gets the next lead).
- **Partners log in to their own portal** to see their leads and balance, and to request a replacement for a bad lead within 72 hours. You approve or reject with one click.

You can still keep some leads for yourself: anything below a partner's minimum tier, outside every partner's area, or arriving when no one has balance stays with CPP Holdings LLC.

## How you get paid (and why it's per lead, not per closing)

You're paid a **flat fee per delivered lead**, from a balance the partner prepays, whether or not it closes.

That structure is required, not just convenient:
- **Mortgage:** federal RESPA Section 8 prohibits paying for referrals of mortgage business. A flat, fair-market fee for a marketing service (a verified lead), not tied to whether a loan closes, is the accepted approach.
- **Real estate:** South Carolina generally doesn't allow licensees to pay unlicensed people a share of a commission or a fee that depends on a closing. A flat marketing fee per lead is the usual structure, but the partner's **broker-in-charge** has to approve it.

So never agree to "a cut of the commission" or "a bonus if it closes." Charge per lead.

## "What if the customer goes straight to my partner?"

That's expected, and you're already paid. Delivery happens only after the partner has paid for it: the lead price comes out of their **prepaid balance** the moment it's delivered. There's nothing to chase. If their balance runs out, leads simply stop going to them (and stay with you) until they top up.

## Pilot offer and pricing

Start each partner with **5 free leads** (Admin → Partners → Give 5 free pilot leads). If they like them:

| Lead type | Suggested starting price per delivered lead |
|---|---|
| Real estate: seller (timeline under 6 months) | $50–$100 |
| Real estate: buyer (pre-approved or cash) | $25–$50 |
| Mortgage: purchase or refinance (timeline under 3 months) | $40–$75 |

Raise prices once you know the partner's close rate. Exclusive, local leads with full answers, delivered in seconds, are worth more than the shared internet leads they buy elsewhere. Tier pricing (e.g. A $80, B $50) lets you charge more for the best ones.

## One-page lead agreement (template)

Fill in the brackets, then have an attorney review it.

> **Lead Delivery Agreement**
>
> This agreement is between **[Your S-Corp legal name]** ("Provider") and **[Partner name, company, license/NMLS #]** ("Partner"), effective **[date]**.
>
> 1. **Service.** Provider markets online and delivers to Partner inquiries from consumers interested in **[buying/selling real estate | mortgage financing]** in **[cities/areas]**, where the consumer has given written consent naming Partner.
> 2. **Fee.** Partner pays Provider **$[amount]** per delivered inquiry (or the tier prices in Schedule A). The fee is for Provider's marketing and verification service. It is **not** contingent on any closing, commission, loan or transaction, and is not a share of any commission.
> 3. **Payment.** Partner prepays a balance. Provider delivers inquiries only while the balance covers the fee, and Partner can see deliveries and balance in the partner portal.
> 4. **Exclusivity.** Each inquiry is delivered to one partner only, and the same consumer is not resold for **[30/60] days**.
> 5. **Replacements.** If a delivered consumer has a wrong or disconnected number, is a duplicate of a consumer Partner already had in the prior 90 days, or is already under contract with another agent or lender, Partner may request a replacement in the portal within **72 hours**; approved requests are credited back to the balance. Replacements or credits are Provider's only obligation for a disputed inquiry.
> 6. **Speed.** Partner will attempt to contact each consumer within **[15 minutes / 1 hour]** during business hours and honor any request to stop contact.
> 7. **Weekly cap.** Provider will deliver no more than **[number]** inquiries in any 7 days unless Partner agrees otherwise.
> 8. **Compliance.** Each party follows the laws that apply to it, including the TCPA, South Carolina's Telephone Privacy Protection Act, do-not-call rules, and **[RESPA | South Carolina real estate licensing law]**. Partner is responsible for any advice, pricing, rates or services it provides. Provider gives no real-estate or lending advice. Partner agrees to be named in the consent language shown to consumers.
> 9. **Privacy.** Partner uses consumer information only to serve that consumer, never sells or shares it, and honors any request to stop contact.
> 10. **Term.** Either party may end this agreement with **7 days'** written notice. Unused prepaid balance is **[refunded | delivered]** on termination.
>
> Signed: ____________ (Provider) ____________ (Partner)  Date: ______

## Checklist for each new partner

- [ ] Name, company, license/NMLS number, phone, email
- [ ] Areas and price ranges they want, weekly cap
- [ ] Lead delivery email, and their Cal.com/Calendly link if they have one
- [ ] Their promised response time (ask for 15 minutes)
- [ ] Broker-in-charge / compliance sign-off on the per-lead fee
- [ ] Added in Admin → Partners, site rebuilt and deployed, portal link sent
- [ ] Signed agreement and first balance paid (after the free pilot)
