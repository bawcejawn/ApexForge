# Working with realtor and lender partners

How the real-estate and mortgage leads turn into money for you, and how you stay protected. **Not legal advice:** have a South Carolina attorney review the agreement below before you sign anything, and have each partner run it past their broker-in-charge or compliance team.

## How it works ("floating partner" mode)

```
Website form ──► YOU (your S-Corp is the only company named in consent)
                  │  you call, check the lead is real, ask:
                  │  "Is it okay if I share your info with {Partner}?"
                  ▼
           Lead says yes ──► you note it in the admin ──► warm intro to ONE partner
                                                              │
                                     partner pays you a flat fee per introduced lead
```

- **Partners aren't named on the websites.** Only your S-Corp is. Nobody but you can contact the lead from the form.
- **The lead's "yes" on your call is the permission** for your partner to contact them. You record it in the lead's notes ("Verbal OK on Oct 30, 6:10pm to introduce Jane Smith, ABC Realty").
- **You choose the partner per lead.** Seller in Conway → your Conway realtor. Veteran buyer → the lender who's best with VA loans. You can change partners any time without touching the website.

Later, if a partner wants leads sent straight to them automatically, add them to the site's **Buyers** in the admin. Then the form names them and they can call directly. Floating mode is the better start: it keeps you in the middle and lets you judge quality first.

## How you get paid (and why it's per lead, not per closing)

You're paid a **flat fee per introduced lead**, at the time of the introduction, whether or not it closes.

That structure is required, not just convenient:
- **Mortgage:** federal RESPA Section 8 prohibits paying for referrals of mortgage business. A flat, fair-market fee for a marketing service (a verified lead), not tied to whether a loan closes, is the accepted approach.
- **Real estate:** South Carolina generally doesn't allow licensees to pay unlicensed people a share of a commission or a fee that depends on a closing. A flat marketing fee per lead is the usual structure, but the partner's **broker-in-charge** has to approve it.

So never agree to "a cut of the commission" or "a bonus if it closes." Charge per lead.

## "What if the customer calls my partner directly?"

That's what this setup protects against:

1. **Leads reach you first.** Partners only learn about a lead when you introduce them, so there's nothing to go around.
2. **Get paid up front.** Partners buy a **pack** (for example 5 or 10 leads) before you start sending. You deliver from the pack. No chasing invoices.
3. **The agreement says an introduction = a delivered lead.** If you introduced the person, the lead counts, even if they later call the partner on their own.
4. **Your records prove it.** The admin keeps each lead's date, the partner's name in **Buyer**, and your permission note. That's your proof of every introduction.

## Pilot offer and pricing

Start each partner with **5 free verified introductions**. If they like them:

| Lead type | Suggested starting price per introduced lead |
|---|---|
| Real estate: seller (timeline under 6 months) | $50–$100 |
| Real estate: buyer (pre-approved or cash) | $25–$50 |
| Mortgage: purchase or refinance (timeline under 3 months) | $40–$75 |

Raise prices once you know the partner's close rate. Exclusive, locally verified, already-introduced leads are worth more than the shared internet leads they buy elsewhere.

## One-page lead agreement (template)

Fill in the brackets, then have an attorney review it.

> **Lead Introduction Agreement**
>
> This agreement is between **[Your S-Corp legal name]** ("Provider") and **[Partner name, company, license/NMLS #]** ("Partner"), effective **[date]**.
>
> 1. **Service.** Provider markets online, verifies consumer inquiries by phone, and, with each consumer's permission, introduces consumers interested in **[buying/selling real estate | mortgage financing]** in **[cities/areas]** to Partner.
> 2. **Fee.** Partner pays Provider **$[amount]** per introduced consumer. The fee is for Provider's marketing and verification service. It is **not** contingent on any closing, commission, loan or transaction, and is not a share of any commission.
> 3. **Payment.** Partner prepays for packs of **[5/10]** introductions. Provider delivers introductions against the prepaid balance.
> 4. **Exclusivity.** Each introduced consumer is introduced to one partner only, for **[30/60] days**.
> 5. **Replacements.** If an introduced consumer has a wrong or disconnected number, is a duplicate of a consumer Partner already had in the prior 90 days, or is already under contract with another agent or lender, Partner may request a replacement within **72 hours**. Replacements are Provider's only obligation for a disputed introduction.
> 6. **Introductions count.** A consumer counts as introduced once Provider has shared their contact details with Partner, including if the consumer later contacts Partner directly.
> 7. **Weekly cap.** Provider will introduce no more than **[number]** consumers per week unless Partner agrees otherwise in writing.
> 8. **Compliance.** Each party follows the laws that apply to it, including the TCPA, South Carolina's Telephone Privacy Protection Act, do-not-call rules, and **[RESPA | South Carolina real estate licensing law]**. Partner is responsible for any advice, pricing, rates or services it provides. Provider gives no real-estate or lending advice.
> 9. **Privacy.** Partner uses consumer information only to serve that consumer, never sells or shares it, and honors any request to stop contact.
> 10. **Term.** Either party may end this agreement with **7 days'** written notice. Unused prepaid balance is **[refunded | delivered]** on termination.
>
> Signed: ____________ (Provider) ____________ (Partner)  Date: ______

## Checklist for each new partner

- [ ] Name, company, license/NMLS number, phone, email
- [ ] Areas and price ranges they want, weekly cap
- [ ] How they want introductions (three-way call, text summary, email)
- [ ] Their promised response time (ask for same day)
- [ ] Broker-in-charge / compliance sign-off on the per-lead fee
- [ ] Signed agreement and first pack paid (after the free pilot)
