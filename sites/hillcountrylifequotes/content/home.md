## What people here usually compare

- **Term Life Insurance:** affordable fixed-period coverage to replace income and protect a mortgage while kids are young.
- **Whole Life Insurance:** permanent coverage with level premiums and cash value for people who want lifelong protection.
- **Final Expense Insurance:** small whole life policies (often $5k–$35k) for seniors to cover funeral costs and final bills, frequently with simplified underwriting.
- **Mortgage Protection Insurance:** life coverage sized to a home loan so the family keeps the house if a borrower dies.
