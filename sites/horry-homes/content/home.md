Prices across Horry County kept climbing in 2025, but not everywhere. Myrtle Beach, Carolina Forest, Conway and Surfside Beach saw their median single-family prices rise, while North Myrtle Beach and Little River dipped slightly. Across the county, homes sold for roughly 95 to 98 percent of their list price. In a market that uneven, the value of your home depends heavily on your street, your home's condition and what's sold nearby recently.

## Selling in Horry County

Online estimates can be far off for coastal homes, condos and newer construction. A licensed agent looks at recent sales nearby, your home's condition and current demand. Sellers also have local questions worth asking up front, such as how flood zones, second-home property taxes or short-term rental rules affect buyers.

## Buying in Horry County

The Grand Strand offers very different neighborhoods within a short drive: walkable beach communities, newer subdivisions inland, and smaller towns like Loris and Aynor where prices are among the most affordable in the county. A local agent can help you compare commute, insurance costs and resale value, not just list price.
