Myrtle Beach has become one of the most popular places in South Carolina to retire. The city now has about 9,100 residents age 65 and older, and roughly nine in ten of the older newcomers moved here from another state. A move like that is a good moment to check that your final arrangements are covered, especially if a life insurance policy from a former employer didn't come with you.

## Why people in Myrtle Beach look into final expense coverage

- **A work policy ended at retirement.** Group life insurance usually stops when you leave the job.
- **Family lives far away.** When children and grandchildren live in other states, having the funeral paid for avoids a scramble over costs from a distance.
- **Savings are meant for something else.** Many retirees would rather leave savings to family than spend it on funeral and final bills.

Nationally, the median funeral with viewing and burial cost $8,300 in the National Funeral Directors Association's 2023 study. Local costs vary by funeral home and by the choices your family makes.

## How it works

Final expense insurance is a small whole life policy, often $5,000 to $35,000. Many carriers use a health questionnaire instead of a medical exam. The premium generally stays level, and coverage lasts for life as long as premiums are paid.

## What to have ready

You can get quotes with just your date of birth, height and weight, tobacco use and basic health history. If you decide to apply, it helps to have:

- a list of your medications,
- your doctor's name (for example, if you're seen at Grand Strand Medical Center or a nearby practice),
- the name and date of birth of the person you want to receive the benefit.
