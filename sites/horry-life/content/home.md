In the 2026 Insurance Barometer Study from LIMRA and Life Happens, about three in four Americans overestimated the price, and nearly half admitted their guess was a "gut feeling." The fastest way to find out what coverage really costs you is to get a real quote.

Grand Strand Life Quotes is run by a licensed South Carolina life insurance agent who lives and works in Horry County. You answer a few questions online, and your agent comes back with actual prices from several carriers, so the first conversation is about options rather than paperwork.

## How it works

1. **Answer about 3 minutes of questions.** Your age, build, tobacco use and basic health history are what insurers use to set prices. There's no medical exam to get a quote.
2. **Your agent compares carriers.** Different insurers treat the same health history very differently. A licensed agent can shop several at once.
3. **You get a short call with real numbers.** Pick a time that suits you. Look over two or three options and decide on your own schedule. There is no obligation.

## Coverage people here ask about most

- **Final expense insurance** for funeral costs and final bills, popular with the many retirees who have moved to the Grand Strand.
