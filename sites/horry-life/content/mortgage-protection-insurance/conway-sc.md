Mortgage protection life insurance makes sure that if a borrower dies, the family has the money to pay off the house or keep making the payments.

Mortgage protection is a good fit if you:

Families who came to Conway for Coastal Carolina University, Conway Medical Center or the county government often fit this picture: settled, working, and paying a mortgage on a single household budget.

## How it works

Mortgage protection is usually a **term life insurance** policy sized to your loan balance and set to last about as long as your mortgage, such as 20 or 30 years. If you pass away during that time, the benefit is paid **to your family, not to the bank**. They decide whether to pay off the mortgage, keep making payments, or use some of it for other needs.

That's different from **PMI** (private mortgage insurance), which some lenders require. PMI protects the lender if you stop paying. It does nothing for your family.

## What affects the cost

- your age and health,
- tobacco use,
- the coverage amount and how long you need it.

Because the coverage is tied to a big number like a mortgage, it's worth comparing several carriers. The difference between them can be meaningful.
